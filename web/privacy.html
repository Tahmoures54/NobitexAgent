Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Data We Collect

  • Email address (for account identification)
  • Hashed password (bcrypt, never stored in plaintext)
  • Paper trading activity on your account
  • Basic technical logs (IP, user-agent) for security

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your real exchange API keys.
  • We do not access your crypto wallets.
  • We do not run KYC or request identity documents.

3. How We Use Data

Your data is used solely to operate the Service: authenticate you, track your paper trades, enforce plan quotas, and protect the platform from abuse. We do not sell your data.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to store your login token and user preferences. No third-party tracking cookies are used.

5. Data Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting support.

6. Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Sessions use signed JWTs. Sensitive operations are audit-logged. However, no system is 100% secure — use a unique password.

Questions? support@example.com
